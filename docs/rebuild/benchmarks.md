# Benchmarks — 2026-11-09 (Playwright, 1440×900 and 390×844)

Captures: `scratchpad/bench/*-{1440,390}-{hero,full}.jpg` (not committed). Lessons only. No code, copy, images or signature effects are copied.

## strava.com
**Does well**
- At 390 the product proof comes straight after the hero: a real route map with a stats card (distance/time/pace) shows what you get before any feature list.
- Plain voice ("A no BS network.", "Start by sweating.").
- One repeated CTA ("Join Us Now") with no competing actions.

**Fails**
- The logged-out desktop home is one 948px sign-up wall: three equal-weight buttons and no story. You can't evaluate the product before handing over an identity.
- Mobile is a long run of centred, equal-weight blocks. The three feature blocks are repeated almost word for word in a later section.
- Uses claims we can't make ("100 million active people"). APEX has no counts, so proof has to come from the product.

## revolut.com
**Does well**
- The hero is one person plus one floating product card (balance, "Accounts"): a person and the real UI read in a single glance.
- The plan grid is very scannable: name, price/month and a one-line promise. Our single AED 49.99 tier can borrow that clarity.
- "Download the app" is the only hero CTA.

**Fails**
- Proof is a wall of award badges. We have none, and the APEX brief forbids it.
- On desktop, scroll-scrubbed product scenes render as **blank black panels** until they're scrubbed (captured full-page). Fragile, and the content is invisible without JS/motion.
- The legal and footer link farm is longer than the actual pitch.

## copilot.money (premium app-first)
**Does well**
- Each feature is a real UI fragment in context (a transaction-fix card, a chat bubble), not an icon tile. Every section has one idea.
- Product-specific microcopy carries the voice.
- One simple plan card with clear included/coming-soon rows. Honest about "Coming this fall".

**Fails**
- Pastel gradient cards repeat for 5+ sections, so it loses rhythm and every section feels the same.
- At 390 the hero phone is small and the "works with" logo marquee crops off-screen.
- Leans on ratings, press logos and influencer quotes. Again, not available to us.

## Takeaways for APEX
1. Proof comes from the real app: screenshots and real UI fragments (Ride Card XP, FLY speed, the APEX Card QR), never counts or badges.
2. Use one CTA pair (App Store + Android) and repeat it. Partners get their own clearly separate band.
3. Vary section rhythm. Don't repeat one card treatment down the page.
4. All content stays visible without JS. Motion only enhances.
